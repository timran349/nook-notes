Nook Notes

Small notes. Always within reach.

A tiny sticky-notes app that lives quietly
in the bottom-left corner of your Mac.

Download for Mac macOS 13+ • Universal (Apple Silicon & Intel)

Hover

Cursor approaches the bottom-left corner and the tiny tab peeks up.

Click

Opens into a compact, translucent macOS utility panel.

Write

Instant note creation, continuous auto-save, and zero friction.

Keyboard Shortcuts

⌘ ⇧ Space Open / Collapse Nook
⌘ N New note
⌘ F Search notes
Esc Close panel
